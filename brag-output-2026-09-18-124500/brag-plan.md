# Brag Plan: Swahili Dishes

## What is this app?
An AI-powered Swahili food ecommerce platform where you can browse authentic Kenyan coastal dishes, ask an AI chatbot "show me what pilau looks like" and get a real photo back, then order and pay with M-Pesa.

## The angle
Most food apps just show you a menu. This one has an AI that shows you the actual dish when you ask about it — then lets you pay with M-Pesa. It's like having a Kenyan auntie who knows exactly what you want and can also take your money.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A single word "PILAU" slams in with a gorgeous food photo behind it. The viewer sees the dish before they can look away.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The hero carousel of dishes slides in one by one: Pilau, Nyama Choma, Samaki — each with a price and a one-liner description
- The AI chatbot pops up: "Show me what pilau looks like" → a real dish photo appears with the recipe
- The menu grid fills in — category filters, search, favourites

## Outro / punchline
"Swahili Dishes — taste the coast, from your couch." Logo with gold accent.

## User flow worth showing
Browse menu → Ask AI chatbot → See dish photo → Add to cart → Pay with M-Pesa

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: warm food commercial with African coastal energy
- Interpretation: dramatic reveals of food photography, big type, warm gold palette, confident pacing

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #0f1626 (indigo-950)
- Accent: #eab659 (gold-400)
- Text: #faf6ee (cream-050)
- Display font: Fraunces (serif)
- Body font: Work Sans (sans-serif)
- Strongest visual element: The dish hero carousel with gold price tags

## Share copy (draft)
"We built an AI-powered Swahili food platform that shows you exactly what your dish looks like before you order."

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed with subtle cooking/kitchen SFX
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3 (warm, upbeat, African-influenced)
- Music treatment: fade in at start, swell during dish reveals, fade out at logo
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; hero glow breathes with RMS, dish cards gain presence on bass
- SFX posture: sparse; card sounds for dish reveals, soft whoosh for transitions
