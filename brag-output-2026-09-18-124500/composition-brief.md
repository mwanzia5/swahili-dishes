- Tone preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: warm food commercial with African coastal energy

- Angle: The AI that shows you your dish before you order it
- Hook: "PILAU" slams in with a gorgeous food photo

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 3s
Black screen. "PILAU" slams in from center with a dish photo behind it. Gold accent line wipes across. Text: "PILAU ya Nyumbani" + "KSh 850".
Sequential/interaction: text appears with impact
Audio intent: dramatic opening hit
Music: warm beat kicks in
Transition mood: hard cut → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Dish Carousel — 6s
Three dishes slide in one by one from right: Pilau, Nyama Choma, Samaki. Each has a photo, name, and gold price tag. Cards arrive on beat.
Sequential/interaction: 3 dish cards arrive one by one on beat-grid
Audio intent: building energy, each card gets a soft card sound
Music: beat builds
Transition mood: clean wipe → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — AI Chatbot — 5s
Chat widget appears. User types "Show me what pilau looks like". A dish photo appears in the chat response. Text: "Here's your pilau!"
Sequential/interaction: typing animation, then photo reveal
Audio intent: keyboard ticks during typing, soft reveal on photo
Music: continues
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Menu Grid — 3s
Grid of 6 dish cards fills in. Category pills: "All", "Mains", "Grill", "Seafood". Search bar with "Search dishes..."
Sequential/interaction: 6 cards pop in one by one
Audio intent: rapid card sequence
Music: builds to climax
Transition mood: clean → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Outro — 3s
Logo centered. "Swahili Dishes" in Fraunces serif. Tagline fades in: "Taste the coast, from your couch." Gold accent line.
Sequential/interaction: logo lands, tagline fades in
Audio intent: final chord, SFX hit on logo
Music: fade out
Transition mood: none (end)

**Music mood for this video:** warm, upbeat, African-influenced
**Audio summary:** warm beat kicks in at hook, builds through dish reveals, keyboard ticks during chatbot, rapid cards, final chord on logo.
